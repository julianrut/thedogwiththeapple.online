About Me

The Dog With The Apple is an ambient music project created by Julian R. - a rookie artist from Poland.

Julian, inspired by artists such as Brian Eno, C418 & Aphex Twin, started creating in late 2024.

Currently, he released just one album, with another one coming soon.
